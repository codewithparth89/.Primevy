Orders & Shipping

How do I place an order?

Placing an order with Primevy Luxury is simple:

  1. Browse our collections and select your desired items
  2. Click "Add to Cart" for each product
  3. Review your cart and proceed to checkout
  4. Enter your shipping and billing information
  5. Select your preferred payment method
  6. Confirm your order - you'll receive an order confirmation email

For personalized assistance, our 24/7 concierge service is available to help you complete your purchase.

What are your shipping options and timelines?

We offer several shipping options to ensure your luxury items arrive safely and promptly:

  • Standard Shipping: 5-10 business days - Free on orders over ₹50,000
  • Express Shipping: 2-5 business days - ₹1,500 additional charge
  • Same-Day Delivery: Available in select metropolitan areas - ₹2,500
  • International Shipping: 7-15 business days - Rates calculated at checkout

All orders are processed within 1-3 business days. You'll receive tracking information via email once your order ships.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping typically takes 7-15 business days. Please note:

  • Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the recipient
  • Shipping costs vary by destination and are calculated at checkout
  • Some restrictions may apply to certain countries
  • All international shipments include full insurance coverage

For specific country information, please contact our concierge team.

How can I track my order?

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive an email with tracking information. You can also track your order by:

  1. Logging into your account and visiting "Order History"
  2. Using the tracking link provided in your shipping confirmation email
  3. Contacting our concierge service with your order number

For real-time tracking updates, download our mobile app from the App Store or Google Play.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery. To be eligible for a return:

  • Items must be unused, unworn, and in original condition
  • All original packaging, tags, and certificates must be included
  • Items must be returned in the same condition as received
  • A return authorization number is required (obtained from our concierge)

Non-returnable items: Gift cards, personalized items, final sale items, and intimate apparel.

To initiate a return, please contact returns@primevy.com with your order number.

How long does it take to process a refund?

Once we receive your return and complete our quality inspection:

  • Refunds are typically processed within 7-10 business days
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method
  • You'll receive an email confirmation once your refund is processed
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error

Credit card refunds may take an additional 3-5 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.

Can I exchange an item?

Yes, we offer exchanges within 7 days of delivery. Exchange options include:

  • Different size (subject to availability)
  • Different color (subject to availability)
  • Different product (price difference will be charged or refunded)

To request an exchange:

  1. Contact exchanges@primevy.com with your order number
  2. Specify the desired exchange item
  3. Our concierge will guide you through the process
  4. Shipping charges apply for both the return and new shipment

Product Information

How do I ensure product authenticity?

All Primevy Luxury products are 100% authentic and sourced directly from authorized distributors or brands. We guarantee authenticity through:

  • Direct partnerships with luxury brands
  • Rigorous authentication processes by our experts
  • Serial numbers and authenticity certificates included with all applicable items
  • Lifetime authenticity guarantee

Each product includes a Certificate of Authenticity. You can verify your product's authenticity by:

  1. Registering the serial number on the brand's official website
  2. Using our authenticity verification tool on your account dashboard
  3. Contacting our authentication team for verification
Do you offer product certificates and documentation?

Yes, all applicable luxury items include complete documentation:

  • Timepieces: Original papers, warranty card, and serial number documentation
  • Fine Jewelry: Gemological certificates, metal purity certificates
  • Luxury Goods: Authenticity cards, care booklets, and serial numbers
  • All Items: Primevy Luxury Certificate of Authenticity

For investment-grade pieces, we provide additional documentation including:

  • Appraisal certificates from certified gemologists
  • Historical provenance documentation (when available)
  • Insurance valuation certificates
What are your care and maintenance recommendations?

To ensure your luxury items maintain their beauty and value:

Jewelry Care:

  • Store in original boxes or anti-tarnish pouches
  • Clean with soft, lint-free cloth after each wear
  • Remove before swimming, bathing, or using chemicals
  • Have professional cleaning every 6 months

Timepiece Care:

  • Wind mechanical watches at the same time daily
  • Keep away from strong magnetic fields
  • Service every 3-5 years by authorized professionals
  • Waterproof watches should be pressure tested annually

Leather Goods:

  • Store with dust bags in cool, dry places
  • Use leather conditioner every 3-6 months
  • Protect from direct sunlight and moisture
  • Stuff with tissue paper to maintain shape

Detailed care instructions are included with each product.
