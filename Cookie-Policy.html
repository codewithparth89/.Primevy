1 Our Commitment to Transparency

At Primevy, your privacy is paramount. This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your luxury shopping experience. By continuing to browse our site, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

We believe in complete transparency—every cookie we use serves to improve your journey, from discovering exclusive collections to seamless checkout.

2 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our luxury collections, and personalize your experience—much like a personal stylist who remembers your taste.

These files are completely safe and cannot carry viruses or install malware on your device.

3 How Primevy Uses Cookies

We use cookies to curate a shopping experience worthy of our discerning clientele:

  • Essential Operations: Keeping your cart items saved and maintaining secure checkout
  • Remembering Preferences: Your currency, size preferences, and viewed items
  • Exclusive Personalization: Recommending pieces that match your style
  • Site Improvement: Understanding which collections captivate our visitors most
  • Limited Authentication: Verifying your identity for exclusive preview access

4 Types of Cookies We Use

Category Purpose Duration Example
Essential Cart functionality, security, checkout Session / 1 day cart_items, session_id
Preference Currency, language, recently viewed Up to 1 year currency_pref, size_chart
Analytics Page visits, popular products, site performance Up to 2 years _ga, _gid (Google Analytics)
Marketing Personalized luxury recommendations Up to 90 days _fbp, IDE

Note: We use both first-party cookies (set by Primevy) and third-party cookies (set by trusted partners like Google and Meta) to refine your experience.

5 Our Trusted Partners

To deliver a world-class digital experience, we collaborate with select partners who may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Understanding how visitors discover our collections
  • Meta Pixel: Showing you relevant luxury items on social platforms
  • Payment Processors: Ensuring secure and seamless transactions
  • Customer Support: Live chat assistance for concierge-level service

These partners adhere to strict confidentiality standards and data protection regulations.

6 Your Choices: Managing Cookies

You retain full control over your cookie preferences. Most luxury browsers allow you to manage cookies through settings:

Browser Settings

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Important Note

Disabling certain cookies may affect your experience—for instance, you might need to re-enter preferences or see less relevant recommendations. Essential cookies cannot be disabled without impacting site functionality.
